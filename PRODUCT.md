# Pitchline — product positioning

Pitchline is an affordable commentary preparation and live desk for football broadcasters, freelancers, and club media teams.

## Who it is for

Commentators who need a single matchday surface: speaks, squad boards, availability, venue/weather notes, and a live event log that works on mobile.

## What it does

- Prep desk — checklist pipeline Assigned through Ready
- Pitch board — formations, starters, coaches, referee
- Speaks — timed cues
- Intel — injuries, scorers, keepers, penalties, venue, weather, fans
- Live composer — shortcuts + template AI lines
- Export — printable match pack

## Principles

1. Clear Go Live
2. Composer always available
3. Mobile-first
4. Export that prints
5. Templates without external APIs
6. Honest data freshness labels
