# Blank canvas / Manual XI

For lesser matches where AF Official XI is missing or wrong, commentators can build the board by hand.

## Enter

On Match Desk (prep/live), click **Blank canvas**.

- Clears both XIs from the pitch (squad list stays)
- Sets `lineupSource = manual`
- Freezes feed apply (`xiFeedFrozenReason = blank_canvas`)
- AF sync and FotMob Apply will not overwrite the board

## Build XI

- Click / drag squad players onto empty slots (same DnD as predicted boards)
- Live-style manual subs: drag replace on pitch / bench
- If the squad is thin, use **Add player · Manual XI** on the squad rail (creates or reuses a club player by name)

## Badge / pitch

- Badge: **MANUAL — BLANK CANVAS** (fuchsia)
- Pitch grass: plum / charcoal (not Official green)

## Exit

- **Unlock feed** — re-enables AF apply on next sync (confirm: may wipe Manual XI)
- **Re-pull Official XI** — owner: unlock + force Official re-apply (confirm)

Normal desks are unchanged: Official sync still works when not in blank canvas.
