# CoComms FAQ (internal mirror)

Public page: `/faq` · Training: `/training` (signed-in only — Settings / in-app) · Brand: CoComms.

## What is CoComms?

Your commentary co-pilot — you bring the notes and the voice, CoComms takes care of everything else you need to make the match comms seamless. Live feed, dynamic lineups, data viz, Scripts, pitch board, and dossiers on one desk.

## Trial

- **14 days** · **3 match desks** · **card-upfront** when billing keys exist
- Choose at start: **Unlimited** OR **Match Desk Pass** (1 / 5 / 10)
- App-side trial when Stripe keys are missing (see `docs/TRIAL.md`)

## Choose at start

| Path | After trial |
|------|-------------|
| **Unlimited** | Converts to **£22/mo** unless cancelled via Customer Portal |
| **Match Desk Pass** (1·£8 / 5·£25 / 10·£30) | Keep **purchased credits** (not Unlimited) |

## Cancel anytime

- **Unlimited:** Settings → Plan → Manage billing (Customer Portal)
- **Pass / app-side:** Settings → Plan → Cancel trial
- Access continues until trial end when cancelled mid-trial

## Bring your notes / Research paste

Core product. Paste your match prep — CoComms sorts it into Notes, Scripts, and profiles. Keep your own voice for day-one prep.

## Unlimited includes

Bring your notes · Research paste · live feed · dynamic lineups · data viz · Notes · Scripts · pitch board · dossiers · Stats · Print · unlimited match desks after trial

## Pass credits after trial

One credit = one match desk. Credits remain after the 14d / 3-desk trial. Top up 1 / 5 / 10 from Settings.

## Data / privacy (light)

Match desks and notes stay personal to the signed-in account (`session.userId`). Commentators can **opt in** so anonymised notes help grow a shared CoComms intel pool — optional, never forced. See `docs/TENANCY.md`.
