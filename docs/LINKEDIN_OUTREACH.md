# CoComms LinkedIn commentator outreach playbook

Internal runbook for Chris. **Zero ad budget** — people search and messaging from Chris’s personal LinkedIn account only. No LinkedIn Ads, Sales Navigator upgrades, or paid InMail packs required.

**CTA home:** [https://www.cocomms.online](https://www.cocomms.online) (signup / trial).  
**Optional later:** short Settings / Training link to this doc — doc first; no in-app page in this change.

---

## Goal

Get working football / soccer commentators onto a **14-day trial** at cocomms.online so they can run real match desks. Measure replies, trials started, and desks created — not vanity impressions.

Do **not** invent open rates, seat counts, “X commentators already on CoComms,” or other fake social proof in messages.

---

## Audience

### Search titles (LinkedIn People search)

Run both title queries separately. Each routinely hits LinkedIn’s **1,000+ results cap** — treat the list as a large pool, not a complete census.

| Query | Notes |
|-------|--------|
| `football commentator` | UK / Europe / commonwealth wording |
| `soccer commentator` | US / Canada / wider Americas wording |

**How to work the cap**

- Alternate queries week by week so you are not always staring at the same first page.
- Filter by **location** (e.g. United Kingdom, United States, Spain, Italy, Germany, Latin America hubs) and by **past company / current company** only when it clearly signals broadcast or radio commentary — not tools vendors.
- Prefer people whose headline or About shows **match calling**, radio/TV football, remote commentary, or club/media desk work.
- Skip profiles that are clearly coaches, agents, fans, or “content creators about football” with no commentary work.

### Explicit exclusions — do not contact

| Exclude | Why |
|---------|-----|
| **Sports Pro** builders / staff / creators | Product / platform peers — not commentator customers |
| **SportsCom** (and similar sports-comms tooling) builders / creators | Same — do not pitch competitors’ teams or tool-builders |
| Anyone whose primary identity is **building sports media software**, not calling matches | Wrong ICP; high risk of awkward peer-to-peer sales noise |

When in doubt: if their feed is product launches, SaaS demos, or “we build tools for commentators,” **skip**. We want people who **call** games.

## Positioning & copy rules (outreach voice)

Match public CoComms marketing tone (homepage / FAQ):

> CoComms is your **commentary co-pilot** — you bring the **notes** and the **voice**; we file prep and keep the **live feed / lineups / desk** ready so match comms stay seamless.

**Say**

- commentary co-pilot  
- bring your notes + voice  
- we file prep  
- live feed · lineups · desk  
- trial at cocomms.online  
- short, desk-side, human — like a producer ping, not a cold SDR blast  

**Do not say in outreach** (keep internal product jargon out of DMs)

- BYO Notebook  
- Speaks  
- OBS  
- Gemini / AI lab / Intel  
- SaaS-shell language (“platform,” “solution,” “synergy,” “disrupting the commentary stack”)  
- Provider brand names in data/stats claims (see `docs/DATA_VIZ_FLASH.md`)  
- Fake metrics or “everyone’s using us” claims  

Keep messages **short**. One idea per note. No multi-paragraph product tour on connect.

## Sequence

Three beats. Space them so you never stack three messages in 48 hours.

| Step | When | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| **1. Connect** | Day 0 | Request + one-line why (no pitch dump) |
| **2. Soft intro** | After accept (same day or next working day) | What CoComms is, in one breath — no hard CTA yet |
| **3. Trial CTA** | ~3–5 days after soft intro (or sooner if they ask) | Invite to try at cocomms.online |

If they ignore step 2, one polite bump at step 3 is enough. **Stop** after that unless they reply.

Personalise one concrete detail when you can (league they cover, a recent match call, radio station) — never fake familiarity.

## Message templates

Swap `[name]` / `[detail]`. Keep line breaks. Edit freely; do not paste robotic identical blocks to everyone in the same hour.

### 1 — Connection request (note)

```
Hi [name] — saw you call [football/soccer / detail]. Building CoComms for commentators (prep + live desk). Would be good to connect.
```

Shorter variant if note length is tight:

```
Hi [name] — fellow football-comms world. Building a match desk for commentators (CoComms). Happy to connect.
```

### 2 — Soft intro (after accept)

```
Thanks for connecting, [name].

Quick context: CoComms is a commentary co-pilot — you bring the notes and the voice; we file the prep and keep live feed, lineups, and the desk ready so you’re not hunting tabs when the board goes up.

Not selling hard here — just useful if you’re on matches weekly. Happy to answer anything.
```

### 3 — Trial CTA

```
[name] — if you want to try the desk on a real fixture, trial’s open at cocomms.online (14 days, a few match desks).

You bring notes + voice; we handle filing + live feed / lineups / desk. No pressure either way — shout if useful.
```

### Reply helpers (optional)

**They ask “what is it?”**

```
Match desk for commentators. Paste / bring your prep — we file it. Live feed, dynamic lineups, Scripts, pitch board on one desk so you can stay on the call. Trial: cocomms.online
```

**They ask price**

```
Trial first (14 days). After that either Unlimited (~£22/mo) or Match Desk Pass packs — pick at signup. Detail on the site / pricing page.
```

**Not interested / wrong person**

```
Appreciate you saying so — thanks for the time. All the best with the calls.
```

## Daily / weekly rhythm (zero budget)

Suggested cadence (adjust to Chris’s capacity):

| Cadence | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Daily (light) | 10–20 connect requests from one title/location slice |
| On accepts | Soft intro within 24h |
| Mid-week | Trial CTAs to warm accepts from prior days |
| Weekly | Review tracker; pause anyone who said no; refill from the other title query |

Stay inside LinkedIn’s normal-use limits. If LinkedIn throttles invites, **slow down** — do not automate or scrape.

## Compliance & partnerships

- Do **not** imply CoComms is affiliated with Spalk in connection notes.
- **Spalk partnership conversation comes later** — after CoComms has **signed-up commentators** and a clearer usage story. Outreach now is direct commentator → trial, not talent-marketplace harvesting.

### General

- Manual LinkedIn use only (Chris’s account). No bots, no bulk scrapers, no purchased commentator lists from opaque sources.
- Respect “not interested.” Mark `do_not_contact` and move on.
- No fake stats, no invented logos/customers in DMs.
- Exclude Sports Pro / SportsCom builders and creators (above).

## Success signals (honest)

Track only what you can see:

1. Accept rate on connects  
2. Reply rate after soft intro  
3. Clicks / signups you can attribute (UTM optional: `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=commentator`)  
4. Trials that create a real match desk  

Improve templates from real replies — not from invented benchmarks.
